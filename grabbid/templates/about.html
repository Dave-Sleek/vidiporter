About Vidiporter

Fast, anonymous public video downloading

Vidiporter is a lightweight web tool built for journalists, researchers, editors, and everyday users who need an offline copy of publicly shared social media videos.

Our Design Philosophy

Most online video downloaders are cluttered with pop-ups, misleading download buttons, forced logins, or tracking scripts. Vidiporter takes the opposite approach: a single clean interface, transparent format selection, zero accounts, and zero database tracking.

How It Works Under the Hood

Vidiporter pairs a lightweight backend with yt-dlp for metadata extraction and ffmpeg for lossless container merging. When you request a video, the server fetches only the stream you selected into an isolated temporary folder, streams it to your browser, and deletes the temporary folder immediately.

Supported Networks

Public videos from Facebook, Instagram, X (Twitter), LinkedIn, Threads, TikTok, Pinterest, Snapchat, Twitch clips, and hundreds of other public media sites.
