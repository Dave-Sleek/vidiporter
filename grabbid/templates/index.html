Capabilities

Built for modern social video formats

Powered by yt-dlp and ffmpeg to handle adaptive streams, reels, clips, and audio extraction reliably.

Automatic HD MP4 merging

Platforms like Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok often separate 1080p video from audio. Vidiporter automatically combines both tracks using ffmpeg into one playable MP4 file.

100% anonymous & zero storage

No accounts, no history logs, and no database. Temporary download files are deleted immediately after streaming to your browser.

Audio-only extraction

Need just the soundtrack, speech, or podcast clip? Pick an audio-only format from the results list to save M4A or WebM audio.

Broad platform coverage

Supports Facebook, Instagram Reels & IGTV, X (Twitter), LinkedIn, Threads, TikTok, Pinterest, Snapchat, Twitch clips, and hundreds more.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Vidiporter

Is Vidiporter free and anonymous to use?

Yes. Vidiporter requires no account, no login, and no personal details. We do not store your pasted URLs or keep a download history.

Which social media platforms are supported?

Vidiporter supports public videos from Facebook, Instagram (Reels, posts, IGTV), X (Twitter), LinkedIn, Threads, TikTok, Pinterest, Snapchat, Twitch clips, and most public video sites supported by yt-dlp.

Can I download private or login-only videos?

No. Vidiporter only works with publicly accessible content and never bypasses privacy controls, paywalls, or DRM protection.

Why does an Instagram or LinkedIn link sometimes fail?

Some platforms rate-limit unauthenticated server requests or require a signed-in browser session for certain posts. If you self-host Vidiporter, adding an optional cookies.txt file improves reliability on those platforms.

Why does 'Video + Auto Audio' take a few seconds before downloading?

High-definition streams (such as 1080p or 4K) are delivered as separate video and audio tracks by many platforms. Vidiporter downloads both and merges them with ffmpeg into a single MP4 before sending it to your browser.

Where are downloaded files saved?

Files are saved directly to your browser's default Downloads folder. Temporary processing files on the server are automatically removed as soon as the transfer finishes.
